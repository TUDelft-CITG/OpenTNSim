Code of Conduct
===============


This code of conduct aims to foster an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment for 
everyone contributing to `OpenTNSim`. By participating, you help create a welcoming 
space where ideas thrive and everyone can learn and grow.

* Be respectful, inclusive, and welcoming to everyone in the project community.
* Communicate clearly and kindly; assume best intent and seek to understand before reacting.
* Welcome newcomers; offer guidance, share resources, and explain decisions transparently.
* Maintain a harassment-free environment; reject discrimination, harassment, and retaliation.
* Focus on ideas and code, not individuals; critique constructively and with empathy.
* Be mindful of privacy and security; handle user data responsibly and avoid sharing sensitive information.
* Follow the project's guidelines for contributions, testing, and documentation; keep changes well-scoped and documented.
* Report issues and conflicts respectfully; use the issue tracker and PR reviews to improve the project, and involve maintainers when needed.
* Acknowledge and credit contributions; celebrate collaboration and strive to improve together.
